Barnet Roleplay — Server Rules

Please read the following rules carefully. By remaining in the community you agree to follow them.

Age Requirement

This community is strictly 13+. By joining the server, you confirm that you are at least 13 years old and comply with Discord’s Terms of Service.

Respect

All members must treat others with respect at all times. Harassment, discrimination, toxicity, or excessive disrespect towards members or staff will not be tolerated.

Serious Roleplay

This server is based around realistic UK roleplay. Unrealistic behaviour, trolling, or intentionally ruining scenes is prohibited.

Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Killing or attacking players without a valid roleplay reason is strictly forbidden.

Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Using vehicles to intentionally harm players or disrupt scenes is prohibited.

Fail Roleplay (FailRP)

Players must act realistically in all situations. Examples include:

  • Unrealistic driving
  • Ignoring injuries
  • Fearless behaviour during dangerous scenes
  • Unrealistic pursuits or escapes

FearRP

Value your character’s life at all times. If faced with a realistic threat, you are expected to comply appropriately.

Combat Logging

Leaving the server to avoid roleplay, arrest, moderation, or consequences is prohibited.

Metagaming

Using information obtained outside of roleplay to benefit your character is not allowed.

Powergaming

Forcing actions onto another player without giving them a fair chance to respond is prohibited.

Exploiting & Cheating

The use of exploits, cheats, scripts, glitches, or third-party software providing unfair advantages will result in severe punishment.

Staff Respect

Staff decisions must be respected. Arguing excessively, disrespecting staff, or interfering with moderation situations may result in punishment.

Appropriate Conduct

Usernames, profile pictures, nicknames, and messages must remain appropriate and comply with Discord Terms of Service.

Voice Chat Rules

Microphone spam, soundboards, loud audio, or disruptive behaviour in voice channels is prohibited.

Advertising

Advertising other servers, communities, or services without staff permission is not allowed.

Roleplay Scenes

Do not intentionally interrupt, troll, or ruin active roleplay scenes for other players.

Emergency Services Conduct

Police, Fire, and EMS personnel are expected to remain professional and realistic while on duty.

Criminal Roleplay

Criminal roleplay must remain balanced and realistic. Excessive chaos or unrealistic criminal activity may be moderated.

Safe Environment

Any form of racism, sexism, homophobia, hate speech, or threats is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate removal.

Reporting Issues

If you experience issues with players or staff, use the support ticket system instead of arguing publicly.

Staff Discretion

Staff members may take action on behaviour not specifically listed above if it negatively impacts the community or roleplay experience.

Agreement

By remaining in Barnet Roleplay, you agree to follow all server rules and staff instructions. Failure to comply may result in warnings, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the community.
