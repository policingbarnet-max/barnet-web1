BARNET ROLEPLAY

PRIVACY POLICY

Professionalism. Realism. Excellence.

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Barnet Roleplay ("Barnet Roleplay", "BR", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information relating to individuals who join or interact with our community.

By joining or participating in Barnet Roleplay, you acknowledge that information may be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

ARTICLE I — INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

Barnet Roleplay is committed to respecting the privacy of its members and handling personal information responsibly.

This policy outlines the types of information we may collect, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the circumstances in which it may be shared.

1.2 Scope

This policy applies to information collected through Barnet Roleplay's associated platforms and services, including:

  • Discord server and community systems.
  • Roblox and in-game activities.
  • Applications and recruitment processes.
  • Training and departmental management systems.
  • Atlas Force Management System.
  • Support tickets and moderation systems.
  • Other official Barnet Roleplay services.

ARTICLE II — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Account Information

We may collect or process information such as:

  • Discord usernames and user IDs.
  • Roblox usernames and user IDs.
  • Display names and relevant account information.
  • Departmental membership and assigned roles.

2.2 Applications & Recruitment

When submitting an application, members may provide:

  • Application responses.
  • Relevant experience and qualifications.
  • Availability and recruitment information.
  • Departmental preferences.
  • Information required for training or assessment.

2.3 Moderation & Disciplinary Records

We may maintain records relating to community management, including:

  • Warnings and disciplinary actions.
  • Temporary timeouts and bans.
  • Staff reports and investigations.
  • Appeals and complaints.
  • Relevant moderation logs.

2.4 Support & Communication

Information submitted through support tickets, reports, appeals, or direct communications with authorised staff may be retained where necessary to address the matter.

2.5 Technical & System Information

Our systems may record certain operational information, including command logs, system activity, timestamps, and administrative actions, where these features are enabled.

ARTICLE III — HOW WE USE INFORMATION

3.1 Community Administration

Information may be used to:

  • Manage memberships and departmental positions.
  • Maintain accurate member and training records.
  • Organise recruitment and promotion processes.
  • Operate community systems and services.

3.2 Moderation & Security

Information may be used to:

  • Investigate reported rule violations.
  • Enforce community policies.
  • Prevent abuse, exploitation, and disruption.
  • Review disciplinary actions and appeals.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our systems.

3.3 Communication

We may use relevant information to contact members regarding applications, training, departmental matters, moderation decisions, and important community announcements.

3.4 Service Improvement

Information may be used to identify technical issues, improve community operations, and maintain the quality of Barnet Roleplay services.

ARTICLE IV — INFORMATION SHARING

4.1 Authorised Personnel

Information may be accessed by authorised members of the Barnet Roleplay Management Team, moderation team, or relevant Department Leadership where access is necessary for their responsibilities.

4.2 Third-Party Platforms

Barnet Roleplay uses third-party platforms and services, which may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

These platforms may include:

  • Discord.
  • Roblox.
  • Google services.
  • Atlas Force Management System.
  • Other services used to support community operations.

4.3 Legal & Safety Requirements

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect the safety and security of individuals and the Community.

4.4 No Unauthorised Disclosure

Barnet Roleplay does not intend to publicly disclose private member information or share it with unrelated individuals without a legitimate reason.

ARTICLE V — DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

5.1 Storage

Information may be stored through Discord, Roblox, Google services, Atlas Force Management System, and other platforms or systems used by Barnet Roleplay.

5.2 Security Measures

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information against:

  • Unauthorised access.
  • Accidental loss.
  • Unauthorised alteration.
  • Improper disclosure.
  • Misuse of administrative permissions.

5.3 Access Restrictions

Access to member information should be limited to authorised personnel who require it to perform their community responsibilities.

5.4 Security Limitations

Although reasonable precautions are taken, no digital platform or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

ARTICLE VI — DATA RETENTION

6.1 Retention Periods

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for community administration, recruitment, training, moderation, security, and record-keeping purposes.

6.2 Disciplinary Records

Certain moderation or disciplinary records may be retained after a member leaves the Community where necessary for security, investigations, appeals, or preventing repeated misconduct.

6.3 Deletion

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or removed where reasonably possible and appropriate.

ARTICLE VII — MEMBER RIGHTS & REQUESTS

7.1 Access Requests

Members may request information about personal data held by Barnet Roleplay.

7.2 Correction Requests

Members may request corrections where information held about them is inaccurate or incomplete.

7.3 Deletion Requests

Members may request the deletion of personal information where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, moderation, or legal purposes.

7.4 Other Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, individuals may have additional rights, including rights relating to restriction of processing, objection, and data portability.

7.5 Submitting Requests

Privacy-related requests should be submitted through official Barnet Roleplay support channels.

Requests will be reviewed by authorised personnel and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

ARTICLE VIII — CHILDREN & AGE REQUIREMENTS

8.1 Platform Requirements

Members must meet the minimum age requirements established by Discord, Roblox, and any other platforms used by Barnet Roleplay.

8.2 Information Relating to Children

Barnet Roleplay does not knowingly seek to collect information from individuals who are not permitted to use the relevant platforms or services.

If we become aware that information has been collected in circumstances where it should not have been, we will review the matter and take appropriate steps.

ARTICLE IX — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

9.1 External Platforms

Barnet Roleplay relies on external platforms and services to support its operations.

These services operate independently and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

9.2 External Links

Our platforms may contain links to external websites or services.

Barnet Roleplay is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

Members should review the relevant privacy policies before providing information to external services.

ARTICLE X — POLICY CHANGES

10.1 Amendments

Barnet Roleplay reserves the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy when necessary.

10.2 Notification

Significant changes may be communicated through official Barnet Roleplay announcement channels.

10.3 Continued Participation

Members should review this policy periodically to remain informed about how information is handled.

ARTICLE XI — CONTACT & PRIVACY ENQUIRIES

11.1 Contacting Management

Any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy should be submitted through the official Barnet Roleplay support channels.

11.2 Privacy Concerns

Members who believe their personal information has been accessed, shared, or handled improperly should report the matter to the Management Team.

FINAL STATEMENT

Barnet Roleplay recognises the importance of member privacy and is committed to handling information responsibly, maintaining appropriate access controls, and reviewing privacy concerns when raised.
