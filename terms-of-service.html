ARTICLE I — ELIGIBILITY & MEMBERSHIP

1.1 Minimum Age Requirements

Members must meet the minimum age requirements established by Discord, Roblox, and any other platforms used by Barnet Roleplay.

1.2 Membership Requirements

Members are expected to maintain:

  • A valid Discord account.
  • A valid Roblox account for in-game participation.
  • A professional and respectful attitude.
  • An understanding of Barnet Roleplay's community rules and expectations.

1.3 Accurate Information

Members must provide accurate information when registering, submitting applications, attending training, or communicating with staff.

1.4 Membership Authority

Barnet Roleplay reserves the right to approve, restrict, suspend, or terminate membership in accordance with these Terms and applicable rules.

ARTICLE II — DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of these Terms:

  • "Community" refers to Barnet Roleplay and its associated platforms and services.
  • "Member" refers to any individual participating in Barnet Roleplay.
  • "Staff" refers to authorised moderators, administrators, and management personnel.
  • "Management" refers to the authorised ownership and senior management team of Barnet Roleplay.
  • "Departments" refers to the Metropolitan Police Service, London Ambulance Service, London Fire Brigade, and Civilian Operations.
  • "Systems" refers to digital tools, bots, databases, management platforms, and other services operated or used by Barnet Roleplay.
  • "Roleplay" refers to organised fictional scenarios conducted within the ERLC platform.

ARTICLE III — COMMUNITY CONDUCT

3.1 Professional Behaviour

All Members must maintain respectful, mature, and professional behaviour across Barnet Roleplay platforms.

3.2 Prohibited Behaviour

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
  • Threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
  • Unauthorised advertising or solicitation.
  • Impersonation of Members, Staff, or Management.
  • Deliberate disruption of community activities.
  • Sharing inappropriate or prohibited content.
  • Encouraging malicious activity against the Community.

3.3 Respect for Members

Members must respect one another regardless of departmental position, rank, experience, or permissions.

3.4 External Conduct

Members representing Barnet Roleplay externally must not deliberately misrepresent the Community or its official position.

ARTICLE IV — ROLEPLAY STANDARDS & OPERATIONS

4.1 Roleplay Expectations

Barnet Roleplay aims to provide a realistic and immersive UK emergency services roleplay experience.

All participants are expected to:

  • Maintain realistic and appropriate roleplay.
  • Follow established roleplay procedures.
  • Respect ongoing scenarios and other participants.
  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorised Staff.
  • Maintain appropriate in-character and out-of-character conduct.

4.2 Prohibited Roleplay Behaviour

The following are prohibited unless specifically authorised under applicable procedures:

  • Random Deathmatch (RDM).
  • Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM).
  • Fail Roleplay (FailRP).
  • Fear Roleplay (FearRP) violations.
  • New Life Rule (NLR) violations.
  • Metagaming.
  • Powergaming.
  • Combat Logging.
  • Exploiting or abusing game mechanics.

4.3 Emergency Services Conduct

Members operating within emergency service departments must follow their relevant procedures, training requirements, and operational instructions.

4.4 Scenario Integrity

Members must not deliberately disrupt, manipulate, or undermine realistic roleplay scenarios.

5.1 Department Structure

5.3 Qualifications & Authorisation

5.4 Departmental Leadership
